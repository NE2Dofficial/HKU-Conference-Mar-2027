Understudied Aspects of Usage-Based Construction Grammar Research

Meet Our Keynote Speakers → Meet Your Host →

This workshop brings together researchers working on understudied aspects of Usage-Based Construction Grammar. It explores how constructions are identified and related, how they change through language contact, and what language development, documentation, and typology can tell us about how constructions are used and organized.

Holger Diessel

University of Jena

Holger Diessel studies the relationship between language use and grammatical structure, including the development of constructions and the organization of constructional networks.
