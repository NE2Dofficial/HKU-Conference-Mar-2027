Scenery

Harborfront and hilltop views

Walk the Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade or West Kowloon Art Park for waterfront views, take the Star Ferry across Victoria Harbour, or head to Lugard Road on The Peak for the skyline after dark.

Food

Central · 1–2 hours

Start with dim sum and tea at a traditional tea house, or stop at a cha chaan teng for Hong Kong–style milk tea and French toast. Central and Sheung Wan are good places to fit either into a short break.

More for you foodies

Culture

Sheung Wan · 1–2 hours

Start at Man Mo Temple on Hollywood Road, then explore nearby Ladder Street and Upper Lascar Row. The route includes hills and stairs.
